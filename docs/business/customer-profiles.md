# Customer Profiles

## Primary first paid customer

The initial target is a 2–10 person Web3 or Arc development team that:

- maintains 1–5 active Solidity repositories;
- has regular pull-request activity;
- does not employ a full-time security specialist;
- builds payment, treasury, or private-credit logic;
- wants deterministic CI checks before external review; and
- is willing to pay for private automation, retained evidence, and responsive support.

The likely first purchase is Developer for one owner or Team when shared policies and history become necessary. The trigger is repeated manual scanning or review friction, not fear-based claims that every finding is a vulnerability.

## Buyer, user, and approver

| Role | Typical concern | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solidity developer | Fast, actionable PR feedback | Low-noise checks, reproducible local/CI result |
| Technical lead | Consistent policy across repositories | Gate configuration, shared history, ownership |
| Security advisor | Traceable evidence and bounded claims | Deterministic reports, known limitations, exportability |
| Founder/engineering manager | Reduced review overhead | Usage, time saved, predictable plan limits |
| Enterprise security/procurement | Deployment and support control | Private runners/self-hosting, SLA, security review |

## Secondary profiles

### Independent protocol developer

Starts on Community and may upgrade to Developer for private-repository CI, multiple projects, and hosted report history.

### Growing product team

Needs Team for roles, centralized findings, scheduled scans, shared policy, notifications, and an audit trail. All are roadmap capabilities, not V4 Grant Candidate claims.

### Regulated or infrastructure-sensitive organization

May consider Enterprise when self-hosting, private runners, onboarding, compliance exports, or contractual support are required. Sales cycles and deployment obligations are expected to be longer.

## Explicit non-targets

- Buyers seeking a formal-verification guarantee or an audit replacement.
- Teams requiring unsupported chains or detectors without a scoped validation engagement.
- Users expecting Arc itself to make application data confidential.
- Organizations that require a production billing, SSO, or SLA capability before those roadmap items exist.

## Discovery evidence before pricing launch

- At least ten interviews in the primary profile.
- Repeated usage across more than one pull-request cycle.
- Documented willingness to pay for a specific hosted workflow.
- Measured CI runtime, hosted cost, and support load.
- Clear reasons for choosing Community, Developer, or Team.

These are validation targets, not claims that customers, pilots, or partnerships already exist.
