# Commercial Roadmap

## Current boundary: V4 Grant Candidate

The current candidate provides the local/open product foundation. It does not include production accounts, hosted usage metering, subscription billing, organization billing, card or USDC subscriptions, or commercial SLAs. No roadmap item below should be described as shipped until separately implemented and validated.

## V4.1: individual hosted foundation

- Accounts and secure identity lifecycle.
- Hosted usage metering and enforceable quotas.
- Developer plan packaging.
- Billing abstraction independent of a specific payment rail.
- Private-repository CI and pull-request checks.
- Hosted scan and managed report/proof history.
- Cost, privacy, abuse, and support telemetry.

Readiness evidence: account-security review, metering correctness, privacy/data-retention policy, cost per scan, billing test environment, cancellation/refund behavior, and customer discovery.

## V4.2: team operations

- Team and organization workspaces.
- Roles and permissions.
- Organization billing.
- Central findings dashboard and shared policy management.
- Scheduled scans, notifications, centralized audit trail, and shared report/proof history.

Readiness evidence: authorization tests, tenant isolation, audit-log integrity, administrator recovery, organization billing controls, and accessibility/browser acceptance.

## Later payment and enterprise stage

- Card subscription option after production billing validation.
- USDC subscription option, planned and subject to compliance and production validation.
- Enterprise annual contracts.
- Self-hosted/private deployment and private runners.
- SLA, onboarding, compliance exports, and dedicated support.
- Custom detector and policy packages.
- SSO/SAML roadmap based on validated procurement demand.

## Commercial milestones

1. Validate the primary customer profile and willingness to pay.
2. Measure hosted CI unit economics with private pilot workloads.
3. Ship a secure, metered Developer-plan foundation in V4.1.
4. Convert initial recurring users without restricting local Community capability.
5. Validate Team workflows and tenant boundaries for V4.2.
6. Complete payment-rail compliance and production validation before card or USDC subscriptions.
7. Qualify Enterprise pilots only when private deployment and support obligations are operationally supportable.

Milestone dates and revenue values are intentionally not promised. Progress should be gated by evidence, not release naming alone.
