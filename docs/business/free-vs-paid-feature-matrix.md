## Packaging principles

1. A developer can scan and verify locally without payment or an account.
2. Payment buys managed operation, collaboration, governance, or support.
3. Hosted history never becomes the only way to verify a report.
4. Security claims do not expand with price; no plan is sold as an audit replacement.
5. Hosted quotas and support terms require measurement before publication.
