# Commercial Sustainability Evidence

Status: **commercial hypothesis and roadmap**. VeilForge has no live paid plans, billing system, claimed customers, partnerships, revenue, or revenue guarantee.

## Proposed model

| Plan | Planning hypothesis | Status |
|---|---:|---|
| Community | Free | Open/local core remains free |
| Developer | `$19–39/month` | Hypothesis; not live |
| Team | `$99–249/month` | Hypothesis; not live |
| Enterprise | Custom annual | Hypothesis; not live |

Community retains the scanner core, CLI, basic SDK, local browser scan, public detector packs, JSON/Markdown export, report verification, limited local history, documentation, and community support. Potential revenue comes from managed private-repository CI, PR checks, hosted history, higher managed usage, team workflows, shared policies, roles, audit trails, private runners/deployment, onboarding, and support.

## Target profile

The primary discovery profile is a 2–10 person Web3 or Arc team with 1–5 active Solidity repositories, recurring pull-request work, no full-time security specialist, and payment/treasury/private-credit logic. This is a target profile, not evidence that such customers or pilots exist. The discovery plan requires interviews, repeat usage, willingness-to-pay evidence, hosted cost measurement, and support-load measurement before pricing launch.

## Twelve-month planning scenarios

Developer is modeled at `$29/month`, Team at `$149/month`, and Enterprise as explicit monthly-equivalent pilot assumptions.

| Scenario | Month-12 MRR | Annualized run rate | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Conservative | `$588` | `$7,056` | Planning assumption |
| Base | `$3,997` | `$47,964` | Planning assumption |
| Upside | `$14,705` | `$176,460` | Planning assumption |

Annualized run rate equals month-12 MRR × 12. It is not first-year recognized revenue or cash collected. Scenarios exclude salaries, tax, payment processing, legal/compliance costs, and grant proceeds.

## Sustainability loop

1. Free local/open tooling supports adoption and independent verification.
2. Repeated teams may request managed private CI, retained history, collaboration, and support.
3. Measured hosted value may support Developer or Team conversion.
4. Validated procurement/private-deployment needs may support Enterprise contracts.
5. Revenue, if achieved, is reinvested in detector quality, Arc coverage, security validation, documentation, and support.

If conversion is weak or costs are high, scope and operating costs must contract rather than assuming another grant.
